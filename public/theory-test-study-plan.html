Study plan FAQ

How should I start studying?

Start with a free preview, then rotate through high-yield drills, road signs, rules practice, and a mock exam when you are ready.

How often should I do mock exams?

Mock exams are useful after topic practice. Review missed and flagged questions before repeating a timed mock exam.

Does this study plan guarantee a pass?

No. The plan is practice guidance only and does not guarantee a pass on any real theory test.
