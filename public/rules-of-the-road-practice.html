Rules practice FAQ

What rules topics should I practise?

Learners should practise legal matters, road signs and markings, giving way, speed limits, overtaking, motorway rules, safety, risk, and vulnerable road users.

Is this a copy of official exam questions?

No. The app is an independent practice tool and does not claim to be official RSA or Prometric material.

How are rules questions prioritised?

Some rules questions receive a higher study-priority score when they include legal/rules wording, safety wording, archived hardest-list signals, or core-category weighting.
