Road signs FAQ

Can I practise road signs separately?

Yes. The app includes a road signs and visual judgement mode so learners can focus on image-based practice.

Are the signs official RSA material?

No. This is an independent practice tool and does not claim official RSA, Prometric, or official-provider affiliation.

Why are visual questions marked high-yield?

Some visual questions receive a study-priority boost because learners often benefit from repeated sign, marking, and scene recognition practice. This is an estimate, not an official frequency claim.
