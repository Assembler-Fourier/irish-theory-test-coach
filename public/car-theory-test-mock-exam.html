Mock exam FAQ

How does the mock exam work?

The app provides a 40-question timed mock exam with a 45-minute timer and a 35-answer pass mark for practice.

Does passing a mock exam guarantee I will pass the real test?

No. Mock exams are practice only and do not guarantee a pass on any real theory test.

What should I do after a mock exam?

Review missed and flagged questions, then practise weak categories before taking another mock exam.
