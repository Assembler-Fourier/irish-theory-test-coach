Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 9, 2026

Who We Are

Irish Theory Test Coach is an independent practice tool for Irish category B theory-test study. It is not the official RSA Driver Theory Test, Prometric service, TheoryTest.ie, or affiliated with those organisations.

Information We Collect

When you use the practice app, progress such as answered questions, missed questions, and flagged questions may be stored locally in your browser.

We use first-party analytics to understand whether the product is useful and where learners struggle. Analytics events may include page views, preview starts, mode choices, paywall views, checkout clicks, restore-access clicks, mock-test starts and completions, and whether a question was answered correctly or incorrectly.

Analytics events use a random anonymous browser ID. They do not include answer text, card details, passwords, or email addresses in event properties. If you are signed in, events may be linked to your internal user ID so we can understand learning behaviour across sessions.

If you buy access through Stripe Checkout, Stripe processes your payment. We may receive purchase information such as your email address, Checkout session ID, payment status, currency, and amount so we can unlock access and support your purchase.

How We Use Information

We use purchase and progress information to provide access, verify payment, support users, prevent abuse, and improve the learning experience.

We use analytics in aggregate to improve the study flow, identify commonly missed categories, measure paywall-to-checkout conversion, and decide which learning tools need clearer explanations.

Cookies and Analytics

We do not use third-party analytics cookies. The app may store a random anonymous ID in your browser for first-party analytics and local progress. You can clear this by clearing this site's browser storage.

Payments

Payments are handled by Stripe. We do not store card numbers on this website. Stripe's own terms and privacy policy apply to payment processing.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact support@irish-theory-test-coach.com.
