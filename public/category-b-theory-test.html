Category B FAQ

Is this the official Irish Driver Theory Test?

No. Irish Theory Test Coach is an independent practice tool and is not affiliated with RSA, Prometric, or any official testing provider.

What does Category B mean?

Category B is the car learner-driver theory-test category. This site focuses on car theory-test practice themes such as rules, road signs, safety, risk, and vehicle control.

Are high-yield questions guaranteed to appear?

No. High-yield is an estimated study-priority score based on transparent signals. It is not an official exam-frequency claim or guarantee.
