# Legal Review Checklist

The policy text is an AI-assisted technical draft based on current system behavior. It is not lawyer-approved text and requires owner and legal review.

## Commercial Identity

- Legal trading name configured.
- Operator type configured.
- Registered address configured.
- Business registration number configured or marked not applicable.
- VAT number configured or marked not applicable.
- Governing jurisdiction configured.
- Support, privacy, refund, and security emails route to monitored inboxes.
- Do not publish a personal phone number. If the final sales model legally requires a telephone channel, use a dedicated business number only after owner approval.

## Privacy Notice

- Controller identity approved.
- Lawful basis wording approved.
- Processor list and DPAs confirmed.
- Storage locations confirmed.
- International-transfer basis approved.
- Retention periods approved.
- Complaint route approved.
- Account export and deletion request wording approved.
- Consent-first optional analytics behavior and browser-storage table approved.
- Under-18 learner wording and international-transfer details approved.
- Data-subject request verification and one-month response workflow approved.

## Terms

- Product identity approved.
- Access duration and renewal/extension behavior approved.
- Payment wording approved.
- Account security and acceptable-use wording approved.
- Suspension wording approved.
- IP ownership wording approved.
- Fair limitation-of-liability wording approved without excluding mandatory consumer rights.
- Governing-law wording approved without removing mandatory rights in the consumer's country of residence.
- Stripe Checkout terms consent and pre-contract information presentation approved.

## Refunds

- Refund scenarios match actual Stripe/admin workflow.
- Fourteen-day cancellation wording and digital-content/service classification reviewed.
- Express request to begin during the cancellation period and any acknowledgment wording reviewed before relying on an exception.
- Model cancellation form tested and refund mailbox monitored.
- No instant refund promise unless automation exists; statutory repayment deadlines remain operationally achievable.
- No pass-outcome refund promise.
- Entitlement effects after refund/dispute are approved.

## Accessibility

- WCAG target approved.
- Known limitations reviewed.
- Contact route monitored.
- Last test date updated after launch QA.

## Content Methodology

- Independent status clear.
- No official RSA/Prometric affiliation.
- No guaranteed-pass claims.
- No official-frequency claims.
- AI draft workflow clear.
- Correction/report workflow clear.

## Security And Assurance

- Public security controls match deployed behavior.
- Responsible-disclosure inbox is monitored.
- Incident and personal-data-breach workflows are rehearsed.
- Do not claim SOC 2 certification, SOC 2 compliance, ISO certification, PCI certification, or independent audit unless evidence exists for this product and scope.
- Provider assurance reports are processor due-diligence evidence, not inherited product certification.

## Launch Gate

Production builds fail when mandatory commercial identity fields remain placeholders. If a production launch still shows a launch blocker, do not deploy.

Legal review remains required even when technical checks pass. Policies reduce uncertainty and document fair processes; they cannot prevent every complaint, claim, or lawsuit.
