# Consumer Rights Operations

This is an operational checklist, not legal approval. A solicitor must review the final classification of the service, checkout disclosures, cancellation wording, and liability terms.

## Before Checkout

- Show exact EUR price, one-time nature, access duration, plan contents, and renewal behavior.
- Link Terms, Privacy Notice, Refund Policy, Cancellation Form, and Support.
- Record server-authoritative policy versions and content/product versions.
- Keep Stripe terms acceptance enabled where approved.
- Do not rely on a digital-content cancellation exception unless the required express consent and acknowledgment are actually captured and evidenced.

## Cancellation Request

1. Timestamp the request and acknowledge receipt.
2. Match it to the checkout email and purchase without requesting card data.
3. Determine contract date, service type, whether supply began, and whether any express request/acknowledgment evidence exists.
4. Apply the statutory cancellation route where applicable; do not substitute a discretionary policy for mandatory rights.
5. Record decision, reason, amount, Stripe object, entitlement effect, actor, and policy version.
6. Where repayment is required, issue it without undue delay and within the applicable legal deadline, normally to the original payment method.
7. Notify the customer and retain the decision record under the approved schedule.

## Non-Conforming Digital Service

- First assess whether access can be restored or the defect brought into conformity promptly and without significant inconvenience.
- Where the law permits, apply a proportionate price reduction or terminate/refund when conformity cannot be achieved or the defect is sufficiently serious.
- Preserve progress/export access where appropriate and explain any entitlement change.

## Complaints And Escalation

- Support owns first response and evidence gathering.
- Payment/refund actions require owner or authorized admin role and an audit entry.
- Privacy complaints route to the privacy mailbox and DPC information in the Privacy Notice.
- Security incidents route to the security mailbox and incident runbook.
- Content corrections enter the editorial report queue and are not represented as factual verification until reviewed.

## Monthly Review

- Reconcile refunds, disputes, entitlements, and unanswered support cases.
- Check policy links and versions at checkout.
- Test cancellation email/form, restore access, data export, deletion request, and complaint mailbox.
- Review whether changes to price, product, processors, storage regions, analytics, or AI require policy/version updates.
