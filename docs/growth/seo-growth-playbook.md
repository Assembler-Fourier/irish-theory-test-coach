# SEO Growth Playbook

This plan supports sustainable discovery for Irish Theory Test Coach. It cannot guarantee a first-page ranking; search engines choose rankings and need time, useful content, links, engagement, and a technically healthy site.

## Positioning

- Product: independent Irish Category B theory-test practice.
- Primary audience: learner drivers in Ireland preparing for a car theory test.
- Distinct value: free diagnostic preview, weak-area review, road-sign practice, timed mocks, transparent one-time access, and a visible content-correction workflow.
- Claims boundary: no official affiliation, guaranteed pass, live-question prediction, or official-frequency wording.

## Priority Search Architecture

1. `/irish-theory-test-practice.html` owns broad practice intent.
2. `/category-b-theory-test-ireland.html` owns Category B and car-theory intent.
3. `/mock-theory-test-ireland.html` owns mock and driver-theory-mock intent.
4. `/irish-road-signs-test.html` owns road-sign practice intent.
5. `/rules-of-the-road-practice.html` owns rules practice intent.
6. `/theory-test-study-plan.html` owns study-plan intent.
7. `/failed-theory-test-ireland.html` owns recovery intent.
8. `/theory-test-questions-and-answers-ireland.html` owns question-and-answer practice intent.
9. `/hardest-theory-test-questions-ireland.html` owns hard and most-missed intent.
10. `/theory-test-ireland-40-questions.html` owns 40-question and 35-out-of-40 pass-mark intent.

Do not create separate near-identical pages for every spelling variant. Consolidate related phrases into the strongest useful URL and link to that canonical consistently.

## First 30 Days

- Verify the domain property in Google Search Console.
- Submit `https://irishtheorycoach.ie/sitemap.xml`.
- Inspect and request indexing for the homepage and the ten priority pages above.
- Confirm rendered HTML, canonical URL, mobile usability, and structured data in URL Inspection.
- Publish two genuinely useful updates each week, starting with road-sign comparisons, mock review, and failed-test recovery.
- Ask a small number of driving instructors to test the free assets through manual, permission-respecting outreach.

## Days 31-90

- Expand pages only where Search Console shows a distinct learner need.
- Add original sign explanations, checklists, and annotated practice examples after content review.
- Earn links through the free road-sign quiz, printable study checklist, instructor resources, and transparent methodology.
- Update weak pages from query and engagement evidence; do not change dates without meaningful edits.
- Track impressions, clicks, click-through rate, indexed pages, branded search, preview starts, and paid conversion by landing page.

## Quality Gate

- One clear search intent and H1 per page.
- Original topic-specific guidance, not a repeated template with swapped keywords.
- Visible author/publisher and preparation methodology.
- Accurate current counts and pricing from generated configuration.
- At least six useful internal links on each priority guide.
- FAQ schema only when the same FAQ is visible.
- No fake ratings, testimonials, scarcity, pass rates, or official status.
- Review safety- or law-sensitive wording before publication.

## Performance Targets

- LCP at or below 2.5 seconds at the 75th percentile.
- INP at or below 200 milliseconds at the 75th percentile.
- CLS at or below 0.1 at the 75th percentile.
- No premium question bank or answer key in public assets.
- SEO pages must not load the learner app bundle or full question data.

## Weekly Decision Log

Record the query cluster, target URL, impressions, clicks, CTR, average position, conversions, changes made, evidence, and next review date. Avoid drawing conclusions from tiny samples.
